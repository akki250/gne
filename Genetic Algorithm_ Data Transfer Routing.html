How it works

Chromosome: a route, i.e. a list of nodes from S to D.

Fitness: total link cost along the route (lower is better). Cost combines delay and congestion. Cut links add a huge penalty.

Selection: tournament of 3, plus the 2 best routes always survive (elitism).

Crossover: two parents swap their tails at a shared node, and any loops are removed.

Mutation: a random node is chosen and the rest of the route is replaced with a new random path.

A Dijkstra shortest path is computed only to show how close the GA gets to the true optimum.
